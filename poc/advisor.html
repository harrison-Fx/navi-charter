Kuching Trip Advisor

Plan here, drive there. It tells you when to leave and which way to go, allowing for school drop-off and pick-up, people driving in from outside Kuching, and other trips already planned. Then it opens the route in Google Maps.

Practice version. The traffic here is made up, not live: it is estimated from the type of road, the time of day, school hours, people driving in from Samarahan, Siburan, Matang, Samariang and Sejingkat, and 400 delivery vans leaving their depots at 7:00. Use it to test the idea (do people leave on time, does Google Maps keep our route, how close are the times), not to trust the numbers yet.

    A simplified map: 16 Kuching areas and the main roads between them, positions approximate. Squares are areas around Kuching (Samarahan, Siburan, Matang outer, Samariang, Sejingkat); the Kuching view draws their roads short, the Greater Kuching view shows them in place. In the morning traffic pours in through them and in the evening it goes back out, so a road can be jammed one way and clear the other. School times come from 38 listed schools; whether each runs morning or afternoon school is not confirmed yet.

    Road reports

      Reports last 2 hours and are shared with everyone using this page. Advice avoids closed roads and slows down jammed ones straight away.

      How the test works

      For about a week, alternate days: on "follow advice" days leave at the advised time and use the Maps link; on "usual way" days drive as you normally would. Save the plan before leaving, then fill in when you actually left and arrived. No account needed: entries are tagged with the nickname below.
